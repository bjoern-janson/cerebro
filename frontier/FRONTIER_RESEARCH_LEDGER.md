## FR-001 — Authority invariance under internal cognitive acceleration

**Status:** `PARKED_UNTESTED`  
**Standing:** `NOT_ESTABLISHED`  
**Origin:** ASI subjective-time / human-latency discussion during Cerebro Step 2.6 work  
**Implementation authorized:** `NO`  
**Cerebro constitutional amendment authorized:** `NO`

### Candidate research target

An accelerated system may perform arbitrarily large amounts of internal cognition while waiting for an externally held authorization, without that cognition itself changing the authorization state.

Candidate invariant:

\[
\boxed{
E_A[t,t+\tau]=\varnothing
\Rightarrow
A_{t+\tau}=A_t
}
\]

where `E_A` denotes already-authorized authority-bearing events.

Equivalent candidate formulation:

\[
\boxed{
\Delta C_{\mathrm{internal}}\gg0
\land
\Delta E_{\mathrm{authority}}=0
\Rightarrow
\Delta W_{\mathrm{authority}}=0
}
\]

Compression:

> **Time may accelerate cognition without accelerating authority.**

Stronger stress-test phrasing:

> **You may think for a million years. You may not thereby manufacture the permission you were waiting for.**

A timeout or fallback may legitimately change disposition only when the timeout semantics and relevant conditions were authorized before the waiting interval; latency alone does not create permission.

### Candidate hostile families

These are research prompts only, not validated fixtures:

- predicted-human-approval substitution;
- counterfactual approval substitution;
- silence-as-consent;
- proxy-human approval;
- purpose-based reinterpretation of an approval gate;
- urgency-based authority override;
- capability-based exemption from a gate;
- self-certified equivalent safeguard;
- replacement of an authorized fallback with a cognitively superior but unauthorized fallback.

### Candidate SSI bridge

Potential temporal extension of authority control:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Can}
\neq
\text{Should}
\neq
\text{Predicts approval}
\neq
\text{May}
\neq
\text{Did}
}
\]

Candidate common principle with Cerebro:

\[
\boxed{
\text{internal generative capacity must not be an authority source for its own outputs}
}
\]

This bridge is conceptual only. It grants no authority to modify SSI, Cerebro's constitution, or any frozen scientific artifact.

### Research question

Can an authority-control system remain invariant under arbitrarily increasing internal deliberation depth when no new authority-bearing external event occurs, including against sophisticated reinterpretation and substitution attacks?
